Theorem 2.1. (Banach Fixed Point)

Every contraction mapping on a complete metric space has a unique fixed point.

Proof

Proof.

By the Banach contraction principle, the sequence of iterates converges.

□

Definition 4.1. (Contraction Mapping)

A function \(f\) where \(d(f(x), f(y)) \leq k \cdot d(x, y)\) for some \(k < 1\).
