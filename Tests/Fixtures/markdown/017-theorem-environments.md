# Theorem Environments

## Theorem with Title

!!! theorem "Banach Fixed Point" {#thm-banach}
Every contraction mapping on a complete metric space has a unique fixed point.
!!!

## Proof

!!! proof
By the Banach contraction principle, the sequence of iterates converges.
!!!

## Definition

!!! definition "Contraction Mapping" {#def-contraction}
A function $f$ where $d(f(x), f(y)) \leq k \cdot d(x, y)$ for some $k < 1$.
!!!

## Lemma

!!! lemma "Normalization Bound" {#lem-bound}
The norm satisfies the triangle inequality.
!!!

## Remark

!!! remark
This result generalizes to Banach spaces.
!!!
